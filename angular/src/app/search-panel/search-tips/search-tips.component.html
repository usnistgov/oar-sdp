Search Tips

Keep words together

Put quotes around a phrase to find records where those words appear together, in that order. Without quotes, a record can match any of your words.

Search inside one field

Type a field name, an equals sign and a value to look only in that part of a record. Quote a value that has several words.

Combine field searches

Join field searches with AND to require both, or OR to accept either. Write AND and OR in capital letters.

Good to know

Searches are not case sensitive. To search for the words AND or OR themselves, put them in quotes. Click any example to try it in the search box.
